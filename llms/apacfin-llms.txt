## Internal application estate (not public, not an API)

Certificate-transparency and DNS enumeration found five distinct hosts under `apacfin.in`,
all fronted by one AWS Application Load Balancer in `ap-south-1`, all answering 403
Forbidden to anonymous requests on every path including `/robots.txt`:

- customerportal.apacfin.in — customer complaint portal, linked from the public footer
- partner.apacfin.in
- lms.apacfin.in
- crm.apacfin.in
- apps.apacfin.in

These are the company's own internal/authenticated business systems. They are not a
marketed API product and no contract for them is published anywhere.
